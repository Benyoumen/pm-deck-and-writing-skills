# Example: 8-slide deck outline

**Fictional.** Acme and the Acme Field Sales Assistant do not exist. All figures are illustrative. The outline follows `templates/deck-outline.md` and `skills/slide-writing-rules`.

## Brief

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Topic | Pilot proposal for an AI assistant that drafts follow-up emails and updates the CRM after customer meetings |
| Central message | A four-week pilot with one team can lift same-day CRM logging from 52% to at least 80% without adding work for reps |
| Decision requested | Approve a four-week pilot with the Northern region team, starting 2 June |
| Audience | Sales leadership and sales operations (executive register) |
| Metadata line | `14 May 2026 | Sales leadership` |
| Sources | CRM activity export (last 8 weeks), rep time survey, pilot design notes |

## Slide plan

| # | Surface | Title | The one question | Key figure (unit, source) | Takeaway label |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Dark | ACME FIELD SALES ASSISTANT | Why are we here? | none | none |
| 2 | Light | What this material answers | What will I learn? | none | none |
| 3 | Light | Half of visits reach the CRM | What is the problem? | 52% of visits logged within 24 h (CRM export, 8 weeks) | Largest gap |
| 4 | Light | Reps lose 25 minutes per visit | What does it cost? | 25 min per visit on follow-up and logging (rep survey, n = 40) | Cost per week |
| 5 | Dark | How the assistant works | How does it work? | 3 steps: read notes, draft, propose updates | Approval rule |
| 6 | Light | Reps approve every change | What is the risk? | 0 emails or CRM writes without rep approval | Scope limit |
| 7 | Light | Pilot design and success criteria | How do we know it works? | At least 80% of visits logged within 24 h by week 4 | Success test |
| 8 | Dark | Next steps | What happens now? | Decision by 21 May; start 2 June; readout 7 July | none |

Slide 8 content:

1. **Decision requested:** approve the pilot with the Northern region team by 21 May.
2. Sales operations confirms CRM write permissions for draft mode by 28 May.
3. Pilot starts 2 June and runs four weeks.
4. Readout to sales leadership on 7 July with the share of visits logged within 24 hours, before and after.

## Scan test (bold fragments, in order)

**52% of visits logged within 24 h** > **25 minutes per visit** > **no write without rep approval** > **at least 80% logged within 24 h by week 4** > **approve the pilot by 21 May**.

Read alone, the bold text tells the story: the problem, the cost, the safeguard, the test and the decision.

## Sample takeaway block (slide 3)

> **Largest gap.** Only 52% of customer visits reach the CRM within 24 hours, which means the forecast is built on about half of the activity. The target is 80%, so the gap is 28 percentage points. The pilot goes after it directly: the assistant drafts the CRM update at the end of each visit and the rep approves it.
