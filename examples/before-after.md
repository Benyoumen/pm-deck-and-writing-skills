# Before and after

**Fictional.** All names and figures are illustrative (Acme Field Sales Assistant). Each rewrite names the rule it applies.

## Five weak slide titles

| # | Before | After | Rule |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | The Heart Of The Problem: Why Reps Skip The CRM | Why reps skip the CRM | Slide writing Rule 1: short, factual, sentence case, no dramatic phrasing |
| 2 | Our Journey Toward A Smarter Sales Process | Pilot status at the end of May | Rule 1 and Rule 6: no journey metaphor, state the message |
| 3 | The Traffic Light Of Deal Health | Deal health by stage | Rule 6: no allegory, literal description |
| 4 | Results | Same-day logging rose from 52% to 81% | `plain-language` blocker 6: a message title instead of a label |
| 5 | Key Takeaways And Strategic Implications For Stakeholders | Decision: extend to two regions | Rule 1 and Rule 11: one question, one message |

## Three sloppy paragraphs

### 1. Cliche opener and empty adjectives

**Before**

> In today's fast-paced sales environment, teams face a plethora of challenges. Our seamless, cutting-edge assistant empowers reps to unlock their full potential and elevate their customer relationships.

**After**

> Reps spend about 25 minutes after each visit writing the follow-up and updating the CRM. The assistant drafts both. The rep reviews and approves them in about two minutes.

**Applied:** `anti-slop` sections 4 and 6. The opener, the decorative nouns and the marketing verbs are gone. A number and a mechanism replace them.

### 2. A claim with no validation criterion, plus a personal name and a criticism

**Before**

> Jordan from the Northern team was really enthusiastic and the reps there clearly understand the value much better than the Southern team, who tend to ignore data entry. We believe adoption will be strong.

**After**

> The Northern team logged 81% of visits within 24 hours in the first two weeks. The Southern team logged 49%. Adoption is measured weekly as the share of visits with a CRM record within 24 hours; the target is 80%.

**Applied:** Slide writing Rules 2, 3 and 7 and `anti-slop` section 2. The name is removed, the comparison describes the process and not the people, the vague belief becomes a measured criterion, and "really" is cut.

### 3. A conclusion that only recaps, with a neutral ending

**Before**

> To summarize, this slide has shown that CRM logging is important and that the assistant can help. Overall, there are many factors at play and the situation will continue to be monitored going forward.

**After**

> **Largest gap.** Logging within 24 hours stands at 52% against a target of 80%. The assistant closes it only if reps approve drafts the same day, so the pilot measures approval time as well. Next step: confirm CRM write permissions in draft mode by 28 May.

**Applied:** Slide writing Rule 9 (translated fact, tension, "so what", specific label) and `anti-slop` section 5 (no recap conclusion, no neutral ending).
